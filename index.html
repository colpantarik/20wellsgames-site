about us

20 Wells Games is a tiny independent studio making small games for iPhone and iPad. The kind you pick up for five minutes and put down an hour later.

We do everything ourselves, first notepad sketch to App Store listing, and we'd rather ship one game we love than ten we don't. Ringer: Horseshoe Toss is the first. The next one's already on the board.

Feedback, a bug, a high score worth bragging about? Write to us. We read everything.
